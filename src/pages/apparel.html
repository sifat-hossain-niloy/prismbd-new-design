About Prism

Sourcing the finest, tailored to your needs.

Welcome to Prism Venture, a buying house with a vision that aims to change the perspective of your garment sourcing experience.

Embarking on a global quest, we curate the finest shell fabrics and tiniest details, sourced from China, Taiwan, Thailand, Malaysia, India and beyond, all tailored to your needs.

Our team is built with experienced merchandisers and a hardworking QC team, who work to maintain the required AQL and operate cohesively to satisfy our customers' needs on orders of all sizes.

Alongside providing the best products, we also maintain committed business partnerships. We have built a foundation of reliable sourcing of fashion garments from Bangladesh, at great value. Join us in this journey to experience a partnership where professionalism meets comfort.

Services

From vision to reality: unravelling our services.

Total merchandising solutions from ideation to delivery. We handle every step so your collection arrives right, and on time.

01

Developments, samples & production

From concept to creation, we bring your fashion ideas to life with expert product development, captivating salesman samples and flawless production.

02

Identifying the correct factory

We identify the right factory for each enquiry or order, based on quality, quantity and timely delivery.

03

In-house CAD

Computer Aided Design for pattern making, grading and marker making that optimises fabric consumption. Geometry drivers allow any number of changes to pattern shape and size, per your fit and styling comments.

04

Mastering production

Total merchandising solutions for streamlined success, from ideation to delivery. We provide guidance and scheduling to ensure quality and timely delivery.

05

Quality control & inspection

Our team monitors every step of production, from trial cutting to shipment, with in-line and final inspection to the required AQL.

06

Commercial & logistics

We follow up on every step from cargo booking to delivery at the destination port, with all related documentation to expedite cargo clearance.
